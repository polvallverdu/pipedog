## What it is

LemonSlice's realtime video avatars, delivered through Daily rooms.

Upstream, it's these classes: `LemonSliceTransport`, `LemonSliceApi`, in
[`transports/lemonslice/transport.py`](https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/blob/main/src/pipecat/transports/lemonslice/transport.py) and [`transports/lemonslice/api.py`](https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/blob/main/src/pipecat/transports/lemonslice/api.py).

Its pipedog package will be `@pipedog/transport-lemonslice`.
