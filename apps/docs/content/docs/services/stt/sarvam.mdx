## What it is

Speech recognition for Indian languages from Sarvam AI.

Upstream, it's these classes: `SarvamSTTService`, `SarvamRealtimeSTTService`, in
[`services/sarvam/stt.py`](https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/blob/main/src/pipecat/services/sarvam/stt.py).

Its pipedog package will be `@pipedog/sarvam`.
