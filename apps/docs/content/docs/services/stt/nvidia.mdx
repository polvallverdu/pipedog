## What it is

Speech recognition from NVIDIA Riva.

Upstream, it's these classes: `NvidiaSTTService`, `NvidiaSegmentedSTTService`, in
[`services/nvidia/stt.py`](https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/blob/main/src/pipecat/services/nvidia/stt.py).

Its pipedog package will be `@pipedog/nvidia`.
