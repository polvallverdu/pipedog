## What it is

Tavus' realtime video avatars.

Upstream, it's these classes: `TavusVideoService`, `TavusTransport`, in
[`services/tavus/video.py`](https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/blob/main/src/pipecat/services/tavus/video.py) and [`transports/tavus/transport.py`](https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/blob/main/src/pipecat/transports/tavus/transport.py).
