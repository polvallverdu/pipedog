## What it is

HeyGen's interactive and LiveAvatar video avatars.

Upstream, it's these classes: `HeyGenVideoService`, `HeyGenTransport`, in
[`services/heygen/video.py`](https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/blob/main/src/pipecat/services/heygen/video.py) and [`transports/heygen/transport.py`](https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/blob/main/src/pipecat/transports/heygen/transport.py).

Its pipedog package will be `@pipedog/heygen`.
